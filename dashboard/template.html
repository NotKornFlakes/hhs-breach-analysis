Method and limitations

  • Only breaches of 500 or more people are listed. HHS publishes breaches affecting at least 500 people, so smaller breaches don't appear here.
  • Organizations report themselves. Counts and categories are as submitted, and the number of people affected is often the organization's estimate.
  • Breaches are counted by the reporting organization's state, not where the affected people live. Per-resident rates use 2020 Census populations.
  • Mega-breaches are shown separately. The [[MEGA_COUNT]] breaches of 1,000,000+ people dominate totals and averages, so people-affected results appear with and without them, and a typical breach is described by its median.
  • [[PARTIAL_FIRST]] and [[PARTIAL_LAST]] are partial years. The data runs from [[FIRST_DATE]] to [[LAST_DATE]], so trends use [[FIRST_YEAR]]–[[LAST_YEAR]].
  • Categories overlap. A breach can have several types and locations, so shares can add up to more than 100%.

Data: HHS Office for Civil Rights breach portal (downloaded October 10, 2026) and the U.S. Census Bureau's 2020 Census. Data cleaning, including the duplicate checks, is documented in the cleaning notebook.
